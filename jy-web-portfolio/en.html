04 — HOW WE WORK

From the first conversation
to the final detail.

We keep you involved in the direction and progress of your website.

  1. 01

    Consultation & discovery

    We discuss your goals, required pages and features, preferred timeline and budget.

  2. 02

    Planning

    We map out the site structure around visitor journeys and content priorities.

  3. 03

    Design

    We shape the key pages and responsive design direction around your brand.

  4. 04

    Development

    We build the approved design and review usability across screen sizes.

  5. 05

    Review & launch

    We check content, links and layouts, then launch in the agreed hosting environment.
